CS4306 - Assignment 2 - Skyline Problem
Test Case Descriptions
--------------------------------------

Run format:  python3 assignment2.py <absolute path to InputN.txt> <absolute path to OutputN.txt>

Test 1 - Baseline example from the assignment handout.
  Overlapping buildings of varying heights. Verifies the standard case and
  matches the expected output given in the problem statement exactly.

Test 2 - Disjoint buildings with gaps, plus two buildings that touch.
  Verifies that the skyline correctly drops to height 0 in the empty gaps
  (x=4 and x=9) and that a building starting exactly where another ends
  (x=13) is handled without producing a false 0-height strip.

Test 3 - Fully nested buildings.
  A tall building (h=12) completely covers three shorter buildings, so none
  of them appear in the output. Also includes two equal-height buildings
  (h=3) that meet exactly at x=18 and must be merged into a single strip.

Test 4 - Duplicate buildings and shared left edges.
  Three identical buildings (7,1,5) must collapse into one strip. Two
  buildings share the same left edge x=12 with the same height but different
  right edges, testing that the max-heap keeps the longer one alive until
  x=20.

Test 5 - Larger stress case (12 buildings).
  A nested "staircase" up and back down, a narrow spike of two adjacent tall
  buildings at x=10..12, and a separate chain of overlapping buildings on the
  right side. Exercises the O(n log n) heap behavior with many simultaneous
  active buildings.
